Biotechnology

Using living systems to solve real-world problems. Biotechnology spans medicine, agriculture, and industry — turning molecular science into vaccines, crops, biofuels, and the tools that shape modern life sciences.

Overview

WHAT IS IT?

Biotechnology is the use of biological processes, organisms, or systems to develop products and technologies that improve human and environmental health. From CRISPR gene editing and mRNA vaccines to drought-resistant crops and industrial fermentation, biotech sits at the core of BE Club's scientific mission.

The field is commonly organized into three color-coded branches — red (medical), green (agricultural), and white (industrial). Each applies the same molecular toolkit to a different domain. Explore the field guides below to dive into articles, resources, and club activities for each area.
